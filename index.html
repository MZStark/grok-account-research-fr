Grok Bot · Account strategy

From company name to account strategy.

Type a French company's name. The planner reads its public sources and builds a sourced account strategy: its priorities, its AI maturity, the Grok Bot use cases worth pursuing, who decides, how usage grows over 12 months, and a first message in French.

Why this exists

An Account Executive covers dozens of French companies. Before each first call, they need to know where AI agents fit the company's strategy, who decides, how usage grows after the first deal, and how to open in natural French. This planner does that research and refuses to invent. Built by Mehdi Zerraf as a working simulation of that job.

Method

Built to be trusted in front of a customer.

Facts and hypotheses apart

What the company does today carries its source. What it could do is labelled as a hypothesis and tied to a strategic priority.

Priorities before products

Every use case starts from something the company is publicly trying to achieve, then maps to Grok Bot agents or the Grok API.

Adoption and impact

A deal is the start. Each strategy defines governance, the metrics leadership will track and how usage grows from land to scale.
